Good to Know

Package questions, answered

Which DJ package fits a wedding?

Most couples choose The Signature Wedding at $3,150: a DJ + MC team, moving-head lighting on two totems and 10 uplights, with ceremony audio and cocktail-hour music available as add-ons. Music-first or intimate receptions start with Reception Essentials at $1,750. Couples who want the full production — 4 totems with 4 moving heads, 20 uplights, monogram, dancing on a cloud and cold sparks — book The Grand Experience from $6,500.

Which DJ package fits a Sweet 16?

The Sweet 16 Signature at $2,750 is the favorite: a DJ + MC / hype host team, the grand entrance and candle ceremony scripted with you, moving-head lighting on two totems and 10 uplights. Effects like cold sparks, CO₂ cannons and a photo booth are added à la carte, or all at once with Full Production from $3,500. For a smaller hall or house party, The Celebration at $1,250 covers the music beautifully.

What does every package include?

Every package includes four hours of coverage, a professional DJ with live mixing, a premium sound system with wireless microphones and planning consultations to confirm every detail before the day. Behind that, every event gets the same standards: liability insurance with a certificate for your venue, professional gear with full backups, and early arrival so we're set before your first guest. Extra hours are quoted based on your package.

How does quoting work?

Send us your date, venue, guest count and the kind of event you're planning. Our team texts you back the same day, usually within the hour, and you book a free 20-minute planning call at a time that works for you. On the call we recommend one package and tell you the number; the exact quote — two options at most, with the retainer and balance date printed — arrives the same night, and your date is held for seven days. A signed agreement and a 50% retainer reserve the date; the balance is due 14 days before the event. Premium dates like New Year's Eve are quoted individually.
