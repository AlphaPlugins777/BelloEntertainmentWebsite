Good to Know

Package questions, answered

Which DJ package fits a wedding?

Most couples choose The Signature: DJ + MC hosting, ceremony and grand-entrance music with a microphone, and moving-head fixtures with LED pillars to light the dance floor. Couples who want the full production — open-air photo booth, live sax or percussion, cold sparks, dancing on a cloud and a custom monogram — book The Grand Experience. The Celebration fits an intimate reception or an after-party where you just need a great DJ and clean sound.

Which DJ package fits a Sweet 16?

The Signature is the Sweet 16 favorite because the grand entrance needs an MC on the mic and lighting that makes the room feel like a show. Effects like cold sparks, CO₂ cannons and the photo booth are added à la carte or all at once with The Grand Experience. For a smaller house party or hall with a modest guest list, The Celebration covers the music beautifully.

What does every package include?

Every package includes four hours of coverage, a professional DJ with live mixing, a premium sound system with a wireless microphone, professional booth lighting and planning consultations to confirm every detail before the day. Behind that, every event gets the same standards: liability insurance with a certificate for your venue, professional gear with full backups, and early arrival so we're set before your first guest. Additional hours are available as an add-on.

How does quoting work?

Send us your date, venue, guest count and the kind of event you're planning. We reply within 24 hours with availability and set up a free, no-pressure consultation. From there we build one flat quote around the package and enhancements that fit — tailored to your venue, date and vision, with no hidden fees. Premium dates like New Year's Eve are quoted individually. A signed agreement and deposit reserve your date, and our full pricing guide, with starting prices and this season's special, is one click away.
