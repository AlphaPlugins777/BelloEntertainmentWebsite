Home Turf

Monmouth is where we live

Monmouth County is Bello Entertainment's home base, and it shows in the details: we know the banquet rooms of Freehold and Manalapan, the country-club ballrooms of Colts Neck and Holmdel, the downtown venues of Red Bank, and the shore towns from Long Branch to Asbury Park where a reception can end with salt air on the terrace. When you book us here, there's no travel math and no guesswork — just a local team that's usually setting up early because the drive was twenty minutes.

Every celebration gets the full Bello production: professional DJ and MC, premium sound scaled to the room, intelligent lighting, and planning sessions that map your night minute by minute. Weddings, sweet 16s, corporate parties, communions, bar and bat mitzvahs, showers — if Monmouth County is celebrating it, we produce it.

And because this is home, your event is also our reputation — the guests on your dance floor are our neighbors, and the next booking usually comes from someone who was at the last one. That's the standard we hold every night to.

Good to Know

Monmouth County questions

Which Monmouth County towns do you serve?

All of them — Freehold, Marlboro, Manalapan, Middletown, Red Bank, Colts Neck, Holmdel, Howell, Long Branch, Asbury Park and everywhere between. Monmouth is our home county.

Do you charge travel fees in Monmouth County?

No — Monmouth County is our home base, so local events never carry a travel surcharge.

Can you handle outdoor and backyard events?

Yes. We plan for power and weather contingencies and bring backup equipment to every event — backyard and tent parties are well within our comfort zone.
