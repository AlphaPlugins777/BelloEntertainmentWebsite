Where Monmouth Celebrates

Monmouth County wedding venues we know

From estate lawns in western Monmouth to the Navesink waterfront, a few of the rooms couples in the county book most.

Estates and manors. The Ashford Estate in Allentown sets the tone for western Monmouth — farmland, a long drive and a ballroom built for a full dance floor. South Gate Manor in Freehold, Buttonwood Manor in Matawan and Addison Park in Aberdeen are the classic manor-house choices up the Route 9 and Route 34 corridors, and The Shadowbrook in Shrewsbury pairs a Georgian mansion with its signature ceremony pavilion. Rooms like these ask for an MC who can move three hundred guests from cocktail hour to the first dance without anyone feeling herded.

Country clubs. Battleground Country Club in Manalapan, Eagle Oaks Golf & Country Club in Farmingdale and Jumping Brook Country Club in Neptune give you a lawn ceremony, a terrace cocktail hour and a ballroom reception on one property — three setups, three sound designs, one seamless night.

Red Bank and the waterfront. The Molly Pitcher Inn and its sister property The Oyster Point Hotel sit on the Navesink River in Red Bank, where downtown energy meets a water view. Down the coast, the Spring Lake Bath & Tennis Club and The Shore Club in Spring Lake put the ocean steps from the dance floor, and Branches in West Long Branch is a go-to for a Shore reception with room to dance.

Booked somewhere not listed here? Tell us where. We work with venue coordinators across the county and walk every room before your guests arrive.

Salt Air Included

Jersey Shore wedding DJ

The Monmouth County coastline is where a lot of New Jersey gets married. Asbury Park brings the boardwalk, the Convention Hall skyline and a music-town crowd that expects the DJ to be good. Long Branch has the oceanfront hotels and Pier Village. Spring Lake, Sea Girt and Belmar offer the classic beach-club ceremony — vows on the sand, cocktail hour on the veranda, dancing inside as the sun drops behind the dunes. And just over the county line in Point Pleasant, the Manasquan River yacht clubs host some of the best waterfront receptions on the Shore.

A Jersey Shore wedding DJ has to solve problems a ballroom never presents. Wind steals vocals from a ceremony mic, so we bring wireless, weather-protected sound sized for open air. Beach ceremonies rarely have outlets, so we plan the power before we plan the playlist. Noise ordinances in Shore towns mean the outdoor cocktail hour and the indoor dance set are mixed differently. We've built our setups around all of it — and we carry backups so the ocean never gets a vote on your timeline.

The Shore is also the county's favorite spot for a company party — a summer outing in Long Branch, a holiday party in Asbury Park, a client dinner on the Navesink in Red Bank. Same crew, same production, with wireless microphones for the speeches and an MC who keeps the program on schedule.

All of them — Freehold, Marlboro, Manalapan, Middletown, Red Bank, Colts Neck, Holmdel, Howell, Matawan, Aberdeen, Wall, Long Branch, Asbury Park and everywhere between. Our Old Bridge base sits right on the Monmouth County line.

Do you charge travel fees in Monmouth County?

No — Monmouth County is next door to our Old Bridge base, so local events never carry a travel surcharge.

Do you DJ Jersey Shore weddings?

Yes. Beach and waterfront weddings in Asbury Park, Long Branch, Spring Lake, Belmar, Sea Girt and Point Pleasant are a specialty. We bring battery-ready, weather-protected ceremony sound for the sand, then move the party indoors for the reception without missing a beat.

Can you handle outdoor and backyard events?

Yes. We plan for power and weather contingencies and bring backup equipment to every event — backyard and tent parties are well within our comfort zone.

Do you DJ company parties in Monmouth County and at the Shore?

We do. Holiday parties, summer outings, galas and launches for businesses across Monmouth County — including Shore-town venues in Long Branch, Asbury Park and Red Bank — with full A/V, wireless microphones for speeches and an MC who keeps the program on time.
