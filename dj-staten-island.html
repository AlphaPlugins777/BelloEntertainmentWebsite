Across the Bridge

Staten Island celebrates like nowhere else

Nobody has to explain a big family party to Staten Island. Sweet 16s with two hundred guests, communions that fill a catering hall, weddings where three generations close the dance floor — it's the borough's love language, and it's exactly the kind of celebration Bello Entertainment is built for. We cross the Outerbridge and Goethals all the time; from our Central Jersey base, most of the Island is closer than half of New Jersey.

We bring the full production across the bridge: DJ and MC team, premium sound, intelligent lighting, cold sparks and CO₂ for the entrances, and the planning process that makes the night run itself. Catering halls on Hylan Boulevard, waterfront rooms with the Verrazzano in the window, restaurant celebrations like the ones we've played at Angelina's Ristorante in Tottenville, or backyard parties in Annadale — we scale to the space.

One honest note: as a New Jersey-based company we won't show up in Staten Island's Google Maps pack — but we show up at Staten Island parties every season, on time, with backup equipment and no bridge-toll drama on your quote. Ask for Staten Island references when you inquire; we're happy to connect you.

Weddings on the Island

Your Staten Island Wedding DJ & MC

A Staten Island wedding is a big-family wedding: 200 guests, three generations, cousins who came in from Jersey and Brooklyn, and a dance floor that's expected to go off. That's exactly the room Bello Entertainment is built for. As a Staten Island wedding DJ and MC team based minutes away in Old Bridge, NJ, we bring the full production across the bridge — ceremony sound, cocktail-hour music, elegant MC hosting, premium sound and intelligent lighting — and we run your day like the show it is. Our wedding DJ page covers the full approach; here's how it plays out on the Island.

Staten Island wedding venues we plan for

The Island has some of the best wedding rooms in the five boroughs, and every one of them needs a different setup. We plan around venues like Nicotra's Ballroom at the Hilton Garden Inn in Bloomfield and the skyline-view Above rooftop upstairs; the oceanfront ballroom at The Vanderbilt at South Beach; Grand Oaks Country Club in Huguenot; Richmond County Country Club in Dongan Hills with the harbor in the window; the gardens at Celebrate at Snug Harbor on the North Shore; LiGreci's Staaten on Forest Avenue in West Brighton; the landmark mansion at Casa Belvedere on Grymes Hill; and tented or upstairs celebrations at Angelina's Ristorante in Tottenville, where we've already had a packed room dancing. Big ballroom, rooftop, garden or restaurant — we scale the sound and lighting to the space and coordinate load-in with your maître d'.

Ceremony sound & cocktail hour

More Staten Island couples are saying their vows on site — on the sand at South Beach, in a garden on the North Shore, on a terrace with the Verrazzano behind them. We run a separate ceremony system with wireless microphones for your officiant and readings, curated processional and recessional music, and a sound check before anyone's seated, so every word carries to the last row. Then the cocktail hour gets its own set: tasteful, conversational volume while you take photos and your guests settle in.

MC hosting & a reception built for three generations

Every package includes MC hosting — grand introductions, toasts, parent dances, cake cutting and every announcement in between, delivered with polish and never cheesy. The reception is where the Island shines: we build from elegant dinner sets to a floor that holds grandparents, parents and the bridal party at the same time, blending the classics the older guests will request with the hits your friends came for. Custom first-dance edits, a timeline mapped with your venue, and a DJ reading the room live — that's the standard, on either side of the bridge.

The drive from Old Bridge

Our base in Old Bridge sits a few exits from the Outerbridge Crossing — Tottenville, Annadale, Eltingville and Great Kills are closer to us than half of Monmouth County, and the Goethals puts the North Shore and Bloomfield within easy reach. We arrive early with professional gear and full backups, insured, and we build any travel into your flat quote up front. No surprise fees, no bridge-toll line items, no stress.

Sweet 16s on the Island, too

Staten Island throws Sweet 16s like weddings — grand entrances, candle ceremonies, a court of friends and a family that fills the room. We produce them with the same energy, and we've built a dedicated page for the parents planning one: see our Sweet 16 DJ in Staten Island guide for venues, the night's flow and the questions families ask most.

Good to Know

Staten Island questions

Do you really travel to Staten Island?

All the time. From our Central Jersey base, most of Staten Island is a shorter drive than half of New Jersey — over the Outerbridge or Goethals and we're there, set up early like always.

Do you charge extra for Staten Island events?

Your quote is all-inclusive — we build any travel into the number up front, so there are no surprise fees. For most of Staten Island the difference is minimal.

What kinds of Staten Island events do you do?

Sweet 16s, weddings, communions, baptisms, showers, corporate parties — from full-size catering halls to restaurant back rooms and backyards.

Do you DJ weddings in Staten Island?

Yes — weddings are the heart of what we do, and Staten Island is one of our most-traveled destinations. Every package includes MC hosting and four hours of coverage, with ceremony sound and cocktail-hour music available for every wedding — professional gear, full backups, insured. We arrive early, set up before your guests, and run the night from the processional to the last dance.

Which Staten Island wedding venues do you work?

Any room on the Island. We plan the setup around venues like Nicotra's Ballroom and the Above rooftop at the Hilton Garden Inn in Bloomfield, The Vanderbilt at South Beach, Grand Oaks Country Club in Huguenot, Richmond County Country Club in Dongan Hills, Celebrate at Snug Harbor on the North Shore, LiGreci's Staaten in West Brighton, Casa Belvedere on Grymes Hill and Angelina's Ristorante in Tottenville — plus restaurant party rooms and backyard tents. Tell us your venue and we'll walk you through how we'd set it up.
