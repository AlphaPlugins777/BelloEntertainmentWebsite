Home County

Wedding DJ in Middlesex County, NJ

Bello Entertainment is based in Old Bridge, which makes Middlesex County home in the most literal sense — our gear lives here, our family lives here, and most of the celebrations we produce are within a half hour of our own driveway. Middlesex knows how to throw a party. The banquet halls and hotel ballrooms of Edison, Woodbridge and East Brunswick host some of the biggest celebrations in Central Jersey, and they demand a DJ operation that can fill them — real sound, real lighting, and an MC who can hold a room of three hundred. That's the scale we build for.

It's also one of the most diverse counties in America, and our music preparation reflects it: multicultural and multilingual sets, fusion playlists that move between traditions without losing the floor, and planning sessions where we learn the songs that matter to your family — not just what's on the charts. From a sweet 16 in Old Bridge to a corporate gala off Route 1, the soundtrack is built for your crowd.

Every booking gets the full production: professional DJ and MC, premium sound scaled to the room, intelligent lighting, 4 hours of coverage in every package, backup equipment on site, full insurance, and a free consultation to start. We reply to every inquiry within 24 hours — usually faster, because when the event is this close to home we're already thinking about the room.

Where Middlesex Celebrates

Middlesex County wedding venues we know

From the glass dome on Route 9 to the ballrooms of New Brunswick, a few of the rooms couples in the county book most.

Old Bridge and East Brunswick. The Grand Marquis on Route 9 in Old Bridge is five minutes from our door — two ballrooms, including the glass-domed room, and the kind of guest counts that need sound designed for the space, not just turned up. Down Cranbury Road, Park Chateau Estate & Gardens in East Brunswick pairs a chapel, gardens and a grand ballroom on one estate, and the Hilton East Brunswick is a Route 18 standby for weddings, sweet 16s and company parties that need a true hotel ballroom.

Edison, Woodbridge and Metropark. Pines Manor on Route 27 in Edison is one of the county's longest-running banquet houses, and the Sheraton Edison at Raritan Center brings a 400-capacity ballroom to the corporate side of town. Around Metropark in Iselin, the APA Hotel Woodbridge and the Delta Hotels by Marriott Woodbridge on Route 1 are where many of the area's office parks hold their holiday parties and award nights.

New Brunswick, Monroe and Cranbury. The Heldrich in downtown New Brunswick gives a city-wedding feel a few blocks from Rutgers, with a ballroom built for 400 and the theater district outside. Out Route 33, Forsgate Country Club in Monroe Township is the county's classic golf-club wedding, and The Cranbury Inn's timber-frame banquet hall in Cranbury suits couples who want historic charm with a real dance floor.

Booked somewhere not listed here? Tell us where. We work with venue coordinators across Middlesex County and walk every room before your guests arrive.

Town by Town

Across Middlesex County

From our base in Old Bridge, the whole county is close. Sayreville, South Amboy and East Brunswick are next door; Monroe and Cranbury are a straight run out Route 18 and Route 33. Up the Turnpike and Route 1 we cover Edison, Woodbridge, Iselin, Metuchen, Perth Amboy, New Brunswick, Highland Park, Piscataway and South Plainfield, and nothing in Middlesex is more than about thirty minutes from the truck.

Sweet 16s are a Middlesex County specialty of ours. Old Bridge, East Brunswick, Sayreville, Edison and Woodbridge host some of the busiest sweet 16 calendars in the state, and we build each one around the guest of honor — grand entrance, candle ceremony or spotlight moment, dance motivators, and a soundtrack that keeps the teenagers and their aunts on the same floor. Metuchen's intimate venues and New Brunswick's downtown spaces work just as well for a smaller, more personal milestone.

Corporate events are the other half of the county's calendar. The office parks of Edison, Woodbridge and the Metropark corridor in Iselin fill hotel ballrooms every December with holiday parties, and the rest of the year with award dinners, launches and team celebrations. We bring full A/V, wireless microphones for the speeches, an MC who keeps the program on time and a dance set that gets the sales team out of their chairs. Planning across the county line? Good to Know

Middlesex County questions

Which Middlesex County towns do you serve?

All of them — Old Bridge, East Brunswick, Edison, Woodbridge, Sayreville, South Amboy, Monroe, New Brunswick, Piscataway, Metuchen, Iselin, Perth Amboy and the rest of the county, plus nearby Princeton and the Route 1 corridor. Old Bridge is home.

Where in Middlesex County are you based?

Old Bridge, at the southern end of the county where Route 9 meets Route 18. That puts East Brunswick, Sayreville and Monroe within about fifteen minutes, and Edison, Woodbridge and New Brunswick within about twenty-five — so there is never a travel surcharge for a Middlesex County event.

Can you play multicultural or bilingual events?

Yes — multicultural and multilingual sets are a strength. In planning we learn the traditions and must-play songs that matter to your family and blend them so every generation stays on the floor.

Do you work large banquet halls?

Comfortably. Our sound and lighting scale to the big rooms Middlesex County is known for, and every package can grow with your guest count.

Do you DJ sweet 16s in Middlesex County?

Constantly. Old Bridge, East Brunswick, Sayreville, Edison and Woodbridge are some of the busiest sweet 16 towns in New Jersey, and we build each one around the guest of honor — grand entrance, candle ceremony or spotlight moments, dance motivators and a playlist that keeps the teens and the aunts on the same floor.

Do you handle corporate and holiday parties around Edison and Metropark?

Yes. Holiday parties, team celebrations, galas and launches for companies in the Edison, Woodbridge, Iselin and Metropark office corridors, with full A/V, wireless microphones for speeches and an MC who keeps the program on time. Every package is backed by insurance and backup equipment.
