Down Route 9

Wedding and event DJ service in Howell, NJ

Howell is one of the largest townships in Monmouth County, and it celebrates like it: backyard weddings on acre lots in Adelphia, sweet 16s that fill a country-club ballroom, graduation parties in Ramtown, holiday parties for the businesses along Route 9 and Route 33. Bello Entertainment is based in Old Bridge, which puts Howell about 25 minutes down Route 9 — close enough that we are usually set up and sound-checked long before your first guest finds the parking lot.

What you get is the full Bello production, every time: a professional DJ and MC who hosts the night, premium sound scaled to your room or your yard, intelligent lighting, and planning sessions that map your celebration minute by minute. Every package includes MC hosting and 4 hours of coverage, we are fully insured, and we carry backup equipment to every event so a tripped breaker or a dead cable never costs you a dance set.

Howell families tend to book us for the same reason our Old Bridge neighbors do — we treat your party like it's happening on our own street. Because, give or take a few exits, it is.

Every Corner of the Township

From Salem Hill to Ramtown

Howell isn't one town so much as a dozen neighborhoods sharing a zip code, and we work in all of them. On the south side, Candlewood, Salem Hill and Southard sit along Route 9 toward Lakewood — established streets, big backyards, and a lot of milestone birthdays and communion parties under rented tents. Just off Route 9, Winston Park and Land of Pines are classic ranch-and-colonial neighborhoods where a graduation party or anniversary can take over the whole cul-de-sac.

To the east, Ramtown stretches toward Wall and Brick near the Parkway, which makes it a quick hop to the Jersey Shore venues many Ramtown couples choose for their receptions. Edgewood Park sits out toward the Jackson line. To the north, Adelphia is horse country — larger properties, long driveways and some of the best backyard-wedding settings in the county. And Freewood Acres, with its deep community roots, hosts the kind of multigenerational family celebrations where the playlist has to move from the grandparents' favorites to the cousins' without emptying the floor.

Wherever in Howell your celebration lands, the approach is the same: we learn your crowd, build the soundtrack around them, and host the night so you can be a guest at your own party.

Where Howell Celebrates

Venues near Howell, NJ

Howell couples and families book rooms all over the Route 9 corridor and the Shore. A few of the venues within a short drive of the township:

Country clubs and golf courses. Eagle Oaks Golf & Country Club in Farmingdale is right on Howell's doorstep, with its colonial clubhouse and wrap-around porch. Battleground Country Club in Manalapan and Jumping Brook Country Club in Neptune are each a short drive up Route 33. Rooms like these reward a DJ who understands sightlines and sound levels — ceremony on the lawn, cocktails on the terrace, dancing in the ballroom, each with its own setup.

Manors and ballrooms. South Gate Manor in Freehold is ten minutes north, The English Manor in Ocean Township is a classic choice for Howell weddings heading east, and the Versailles Ballroom at the Ramada in Toms River is a popular sweet 16 and reception room for families on the south side of town.

The waterfront. From Ramtown it's a straight shot to the Manasquan River, where Clarks Landing Yacht Club and Crystal Point Yacht Club in Point Pleasant host waterfront weddings, and to Branches in West Long Branch for a Shore reception with room to dance.

Have a venue in mind that isn't listed? Tell us where — we work with venue coordinators across Monmouth and Ocean counties and arrive early to walk the room before your guests do.
