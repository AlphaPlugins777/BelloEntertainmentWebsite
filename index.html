Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

How many hours of coverage are included?

Every package includes 4 hours of coverage. Need more time? Additional hours are available as an add-on, and we'll finalize your exact timeline together during planning.

Do you act as MC and make announcements?

Yes — professional MC hosting is included with The Signature and The Grand Experience, and available as an add-on to The Celebration. We handle introductions, announcements, and keeping your timeline on track so the night flows effortlessly.

Can we pick the music and a do-not-play list?

Absolutely — it's the heart of how we work. You'll share your must-play list, your do-not-play list, and the moments that matter most, and we build the entire night around your vision. Then we read the room live to keep the dance floor full. You're the star of your party; the soundtrack should sound like you.

What happens if there's a technical issue?

We bring backup equipment to every event and plan for contingencies, so your celebration never skips a beat.

How do we reserve our date?

Send an inquiry through the form below and we'll set up a free, no-pressure consultation to talk through your vision. If it feels like the right fit, a signed agreement plus deposit locks in your date — and from that moment on, we're with you every step of the way.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular dates book months ahead — especially spring and fall weekends. The earlier you reach out, the better the chance we can hold your date. For the full booking calendar, see How Far in Advance Should I Book a Wedding DJ?

What areas do you serve?

We're based in New Jersey and serve Monmouth County (Freehold, Middletown, Red Bank), Middlesex County (Edison, Woodbridge, Old Bridge, East Brunswick, Metuchen) and all of NJ — including Princeton — plus Staten Island, Brooklyn and the greater New York City area. Planning something a little further out? Just ask in your inquiry — we're always happy to travel for the right event.
