The Main Event

Built around the birthday star

A Sweet 16 isn't a smaller wedding — it's its own kind of show, and it lives or dies on energy. Bello Entertainment produces high-energy Sweet 16s across New Jersey, Staten Island and Brooklyn with one rule: the guest of honor is the headliner. Custom grand entrance, spotlight dances, hype moments with their friends, and a soundtrack built from what they actually listen to.

Before the party we plan the whole arc with you and the birthday star — the entrance song, the candle ceremony, the father-daughter or mother-son dance, the surprise moments. On the night, our DJ and MC team runs it like a production: interactive dance motivators, games that actually get teens on the floor, and transitions timed so the room never goes flat.

And because half the room is family, we mix for everyone — today's hits for the dance floor, throwbacks that pull the parents up, and tasteful volume through dinner. From banquet halls in Old Bridge and East Brunswick to venues in Staten Island and Brooklyn, we bring the same production value to every room.

How the Night Flows

The moments we produce

The grand entrance

The whole party takes its cue from the first two minutes. We build the entrance around the guest of honor — their song, a scripted MC intro, the room's lighting dropped and re-lit on the beat, and cold sparks or CO₂ if you want the full concert moment. Court entrances and friend-group walk-ins are choreographed the same way.

The candle ceremony

Sixteen candles, done right, is the emotional center of the night — and it's the moment most DJs just wing. We plan it with you in advance: the order (family first, then friends, then the final candles), a song for each group, and MC lines that keep it moving without rushing it. Grandma gets her moment; so does the best friend.

The arc of the party

A typical four-hour Sweet 16 runs: arrival music as guests find their tables, the grand entrance, dinner with the volume dialed for conversation, the candle ceremony and parent dance while everyone's still seated, cake — and then the floor opens and stays open. We sequence the formalities early on purpose: the last ninety minutes belong to the dance floor.

Quinceañera traditions welcome

More and more of our Sweet 16s blend quinceañera elements — a court entrance, the waltz, a surprise dance, Spanish-language favorites alongside the Top 40. We plan bilingual announcements for the key moments and build the set so both sides of the family hear their music.

Good to Know

Sweet 16 questions, answered

Do you do a custom grand entrance for the guest of honor?

Yes — the grand entrance is the signature moment of every Sweet 16 we produce. Custom intro music, spotlight, and a hype moment built around the guest of honor's personality. Cold sparks and CO₂ are available to turn it into a concert-level entrance.

How much does a Sweet 16 DJ cost in NJ?

Our Sweet 16 packages start at $1,250 for professional DJ-only coverage; most families choose DJ + MC hosting, and effects like cold sparks, CO₂ cannons and photo booths are added à la carte. Every quote is flat and tailored to your venue, guest count and vision — see our packages or get the full pricing guide.

Do you run the candle ceremony?

Yes — we produce it, not just announce it. Before the party we help you plan the candle order (family first, then friends, then the final candles), pick a song for each group, and script the MC intros so the moment flows. On the night, music, lighting and timing are all handled.

Can you keep both the teens and the adults happy?

That's the craft. We blend today's hits for the dance floor with throwbacks that pull the parents and grandparents up too — reading the room live and shifting the energy as the night builds.

How does music planning work with a teenager?

The guest of honor builds the must-play list — it's their night, and we play what they actually listen to. Parents set the rules: clean versions are our default for Sweet 16s, and the do-not-play list is honored no matter who requests a song.

What party effects work best for a Sweet 16?

Cold sparks for the entrance, CO₂ cannons for the drops, intelligent dance-floor lighting and the open-air photo booth are the most-requested. LED screens for a baby-photo montage are a favorite with parents. We'll help you pick what fits your venue and vision.

How many hours does a Sweet 16 need?

Four hours covers most Sweet 16s — arrival music, the grand entrance, dinner, the candle ceremony and formalities, and a long open dance floor to close. If your venue slot runs longer, extra coverage is a flat add-on we'll quote up front.

Do you DJ quinceañeras?

Yes — court entrances, the waltz, the surprise dance and bilingual announcements for key moments. Many of our Sweet 16s blend quinceañera traditions too, and we plan the music for both sides of the family.

Which areas do you serve for Sweet 16s?

We produce Sweet 16s across New Jersey — Monmouth, Middlesex and Ocean Counties especially, from Old Bridge and East Brunswick to Freehold, Marlboro, Toms River and Brick — plus Staten Island and Brooklyn.
