Every Brooklyn room is different

Brooklyn doesn't have one kind of venue, so it can't have one kind of DJ. A converted loft in Williamsburg, a waterfront room under the bridge in DUMBO, a classic catering hall in Bay Ridge, a brownstone backyard in Park Slope, a company party in Industry City — each room asks for different sound, different lighting and a different read of the crowd. That versatility is what Bello Entertainment brings over the Verrazzano.

Our approach in Brooklyn is the same as everywhere: plan hard, produce clean. We coordinate load-in and power with your venue ahead of time (Brooklyn venues love a vendor who asks), scale the rig to the room, and build a soundtrack for crowds that are usually the most musically opinionated in the tri-state — which we consider a compliment.

Weddings, corporate events, sweet 16s, engagement parties, milestone birthdays — from our New Jersey base, Brooklyn is a bridge away, and your quote includes the travel with no surprises. If your venue has quirks, tell us in the inquiry; odds are we've solved that room before.
