Good to Know

Holiday party questions, answered

How far in advance should we book a holiday party DJ?

December weekends go first — most NJ companies lock in their holiday party entertainment between Labor Day and mid-October. If your date is a Friday or Saturday in December, reach out as early as you can; weeknight office parties have a little more breathing room.

Can you handle speeches, awards and announcements?

Yes — that's half the job at a corporate holiday party. We provide wireless microphones and clean, professional sound for toasts, year-in-review speeches, awards and raffles, MC the transitions so the program stays on schedule, and read the room so the dance floor takes over the moment the formalities wrap.

Is the music appropriate for a work event?

Always. We build a clean, crowd-pleasing soundtrack that works across generations and keeps HR happy — holiday classics, Motown, throwbacks and today's hits — and we'll take requests from your team all night. Explicit tracks never make the playlist at a corporate event.

Do you carry insurance our venue can verify?

Yes — we carry liability insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance (COI) directly to your venue, which most NJ banquet rooms, restaurants and hotels require from outside vendors.

Do you DJ New Year's Eve parties?

We do — NYE is one night with everyone celebrating at once, so those dates book earliest of all. Private parties, restaurant celebrations and corporate NYE events are all welcome; tell us your plans in the inquiry and we'll confirm availability right away.

What areas do you serve for holiday parties?

We're based in New Jersey and serve Monmouth and Middlesex Counties and all of NJ, plus Staten Island, Brooklyn and the greater New York City area — offices, restaurants, banquet halls, country clubs and private homes.
